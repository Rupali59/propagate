# Convertibility audit — 2026-08-17

**The question:** which of these hazards can stop being things anyone has to
remember? The most reliable gotcha is one deleted because the hazard became
impossible, and `docs/GOTCHAS.md` had never been asked that question.

Scope: the 29 entries cited by no file under `lib/`, `cli.mjs`, `digest.mjs` or
`tests/`. The other three corpora (job-radar 13, ppk-shopify 29,
`~/.claude/gotchas-global.md` 4) are out of scope for this pass.

## The correction that had to come first

**Citation is not coverage, and the headline number was wrong.**

The 29 came from matching `\bG\d+\b` across the source tree. That measures
whether an entry's *number* appears in code — not whether its hazard is guarded.
Resolving each entry against the code moved **seven** of them:

| G44 | prose-only | **guarded** — `--apply` gates every disposition; `tests/verify-ordering.test.mjs` snapshots the store byte-for-byte around every non-writing path |
| G19 | prose-only | **already a rule** — `rule:delegation-criteria` §2, "prefer derive-on-demand over remember-in-background" |
| G42 | prose-only | **already a rule** — `rule:discernment-checks` §4, which carries 15 rows to this entry's 7 |

**So the real unconverted count is 22, not 29.** Recorded as a finding rather
than quietly corrected (`rule:discernment-checks` §5 — state what the measurement
is over, and check it is the same thing the claim is about).

**Two probes in this audit were themselves wrong**, which is G42 happening inside
the audit of G42:

- *"G38: drain points at verify for derived states"* → **false positive.** The
  regex matched JSDoc at `cli.mjs:71` and `:2338`; the `drain` command says
  nothing of the kind to a user.
- *"G30: ANSI-strip helper shared in tests"* → **false positive.** It is
  duplicated inline in `journal.test.mjs` and `whole-project-ledger.test.mjs`;
  `tests/helpers/` holds no such helper.

Both were caught by re-reading rather than re-grepping.

## What this changes about the file

29 prose-only → **22 unconverted**, and of those 22, **14 get a mechanism**
(2 promote, 3 eliminate, 3 detect, 5 deliver, 1 retire) leaving **8 as
irreducible judgement**.

The pattern worth carrying forward: **the corpus holds the same hazard at several
levels of abstraction, and the general form is the one that goes unenforced.**
G22 generalises G39 and G44; `rule:discernment-checks` §4 generalises G42;
`rule:delegation-criteria` §2 generalises G19. In every case the specific
instance was written down and the general form either lived somewhere that does
not load, or was backlogged and never written.

A gotcha that has a general form belongs in `~/.claude/rules/`. What stays in
`GOTCHAS.md` is the incident — the cost, the wrong theory held first, and the
signal that was visible at the time.
